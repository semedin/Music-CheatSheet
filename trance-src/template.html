01 / MOTIF

Make four notes mean something.

Pick a root, third, fifth and one colour note. Write a two-bar rhythm. Repeat it exactly, then change only the last two notes of the answer.

Try: Original → Singing reply.

02 / HARMONY

Let the floor move beneath it.

Hold the melody steady and change the bass root. Keep common notes between chords. Sing the highest note of each voicing as a second melody.

Try: mute Melody; listen to Chords + Bass.

03 / RELEASE

Save something for the return.

Remove a layer in the breakdown. Bring back the dry groove first, then the widest version of the hook. Contrast makes the return feel large.

Try: Breakdown theme → Rising answer.

06 / FROM BROWSER TO DANCEFLOOR

The finishing details.

Bring the whole idea into your DAW ↗
  1. Set your DAW to the BPM shown in the studio.
  2. Download Full MIDI and import it as separate tracks: melody, chords, bass and drums. The file also contains tempo and 4/4 metadata.
  3. Assign a lead, pad, mono bass and drum rack. Drum notes use kick 36, clap 39 and closed hat 42.
  4. Use the phrase length shown: sources are 4, 8 or 16 bars; developments are 8 or 16 bars. MIDI contains notes, not the preview sounds or audio effects.
  5. Save a copy, then write a second phrase with a different final two bars.
Get the kick and bass to agree ↗

Solo kick and bass at the intended tempo. Shorten the bass release until each kick is clear; then lengthen it just enough to recover weight. Check in mono. If the transient and sub fight, adjust their overlap before changing the whole bass sound.

Offbeat bass lands halfway between kicks. Rolling psy bass typically puts three sixteenths after each kick. The exact envelope depends on the kick tail and pitch.

Choose delay times that reinforce the hook ↗

A dotted eighth can fill the gaps between straight eighth-note plucks. Keep feedback moderate, remove low frequencies from the return, and reduce the send if the answer becomes hard to hear.

Keep wide leads clear in mono ↗

Build a strong centre voice, then add stereo layers around it. High-pass the wide layers and compare the drop in mono. If the main melody disappears, reduce detune or chorus depth and raise the dry centre.

Do the same check with delay and reverb returns muted. The dry hook should still communicate.

Turn a chord into a chord melody ↗

First export the sustained chord voicings. Move the upper voice by the smallest useful interval between chords, then listen to that voice alone. Next try Pulsed eighths for rhythmic chord stabs, or Arpeggiated for a line through the actual voiced notes.

Chord conversation connects chord tones with melodic passing notes. Chord top line exports the highest voice of the harmony as its own MIDI part. Export Melody and Chords separately to develop the two parts in your DAW.

How this library is put together ↗

30 supplied MIDI files plus 60 source-developed studies, each with eight treatments: 720 arranged sketches. Each source has an eight-bar New horizon and a sixteen-bar Ascension. Every sketch has lead melody, voiced chords, root-following bass and a simple drum foundation. Tempo suggestions and style descriptions are broad creative guides.

Your original files stay embedded and downloadable. Favourites and studio settings are saved locally when browser storage is available. No account, uploads, external samples or network connection is required for the studio.
