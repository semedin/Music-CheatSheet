      How to use it

      1. Bounce your mix and drop it in. Play the drop.
      2. Walk the night with →. In each place, press S to flip to the studio and back: the difference is the place.
      3. Read the report. Fix the first thing on the list in your project, bounce again, drop it in again.
      4. Add a reference track you trust. If the reference breaks in a place too, that place is hard on everybody, not on you.

      Limits

      • These are models, not measurements. Every room, wall and speaker is built from typical figures. A real club will differ; the direction of the change will not.
      • Loudness is matched by default. A phone is not as loud as a club. Matching lets you compare tone; turn it off to hear roughly real levels.
      • Your own speakers are in the chain. On laptop speakers the sub stack cannot be heard. Use headphones or monitors.
      • The report reads the loudest 32 seconds and its thresholds are rules of thumb. A reference track is a better yardstick than any of them.
      • “The bassline” is whatever sits on the low note's harmonic series. A chord that doubles the root counts, because it carries the note too.
      Does anything leave my computer?

      No. There is no server. The file is read and decoded by your browser, and only your settings (never audio) are remembered, in this browser.

      Why is the fixed demo mix scored so much higher?

      Because the seven problems were planted on purpose and the report is tested against them: it must find all seven in the first mix and none in the second. That is how this page is validated. See OUTSIDE.md.

      Why does my track get louder through a wall?

      It does not. With loudness matching on, every place is brought to the same level so you can compare tone. The Loudness column in the report shows what each place does to the level before matching.

      Outside is one standalone page. Every room, wall, crowd and street is synthesised in your browser; there are no audio files in it. Notes: OUTSIDE.md.
