A SMALL GUIDE TO GETTING LOST

Start with something
you want to hear again.

  1. Choose a style or artist perspective and press New idea. Filter the list by family, search for an artist, or use the perspective chips under the style name.
  2. Play the two-bar motif with the groove. Try a simple preview sound.
  3. Click a note to edit its pitch, time, length or velocity. Shift-click protects it from development.
  4. Audition a rhythm, pitch or ending alternative. Keep the version that says something to you.
  5. Switch to the full phrase. Hear the motif repeat over moving chords.
  6. Export the melody or idea family into Ableton. Load your instruments and check tempo and loop brackets.

Space: play / stop. Ctrl+Z: undo. Ctrl+Shift+Z: redo. Delete: remove the selected note. Editing controls affect the two-bar source motif.
