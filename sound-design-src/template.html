05 / BUILD THE SKILL

Eight weeks. Your own sounds.

25 minutes a day, five days a week.
Move on when you can demonstrate the skill.

Copy → change → recall → use.

Build once with the recipe open. Change one thing and predict the result. Next day, rebuild from Init with the recipe closed. Finish the week by using the sound in an eight-bar loop. Progress depends on deliberate listening, not just time spent.

Check a session only after doing its listening task. Recipe “recalled” marks are separate from these sessions.
